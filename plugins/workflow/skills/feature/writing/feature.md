# Feature

Read this before you write or change `feature.md`.

`feature.md` answers what should change, why it matters and where the boundary is.
A reader who knows nothing about the project sees at once what we want and why.

- Keep it under 100 lines
- Keep the template's sections in their order
- Use `Details` only to link where the detailed rules live
- Add `Assumptions`, `Contracts` or `Open questions` only when they carry information
- Resolve an open question into the relevant section or remove it
